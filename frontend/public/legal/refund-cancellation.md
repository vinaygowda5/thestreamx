###### Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** 2026

Thank you for subscribing to StreamX. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for subscription payments made on our platform.

###### Cancellation Window

You may request to cancel your subscription and receive a full refund **within 2 hours of completing payment**.

###### After the Cancellation Window

Once more than 2 hours have passed since your payment was completed, your subscription is considered active and used, and **no refund will be issued** for that billing cycle, whether or not you choose to continue using the service.

###### How to Request a Refund

To request a cancellation and refund within the 2-hour window, contact our support team through Help Center or Contact Support with your payment reference. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method and may take a few business days to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.

###### Subscription Renewals

Recurring subscription renewals are treated the same way as a new payment — you have 2 hours from the renewal charge to request a cancellation and refund for that renewal.

###### Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
