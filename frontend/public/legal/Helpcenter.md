###### Help Center

Answers to the most common questions. For anything else, use **Contact Support** in the footer.

###### A video isn't playing

Check your internet connection, refresh the app and try again. If the problem continues for one title only, it may be temporarily unavailable. Let our support team know the title name.

###### I didn't receive my login code

Check your spam folder and make sure the email address is correct. Wait a minute and request a new code. Only the most recent code works.

###### How do I upgrade to Premium?

Open your Profile or tap Subscribe on the Home page, choose a plan and complete payment. Premium removes ads and enables downloads.

###### How do downloads work?

Downloads are available on Premium. Downloaded titles are stored inside the app in your browser, not in your device gallery, and can be watched offline from the app.

###### How do I cancel or get a refund?

Refunds are available only if you cancel within 2 hours of payment. See the Refund & Cancellation policy for full details.

###### I reached my device limit

Sign out from a device you no longer use, then sign in on the new one.
